5-MINUTE DEMO (one story)
  1. Forward a scam SMS to the Telegram bot -> High, 12 of 57 checks flagged, reasons, advice, buttons.
  2. Open the result on the website: detection meter, Hindi advice, ask the assistant "Is it safe to click the link?".
  3. Snip a scam message on a web page with the extension -> verdict in a new tab.
  4. Officer dashboard: the case is in the queue; the link graph joins it to the APK and the lottery SMS.
  5. Officer marks the UPI ID "Confirmed fraud". Forward a new SMS with that UPI ID: the bot now says
     "Confirmed fraud" – police knowledge flows back to every citizen channel.

TIPS FOR JUDGES' QUESTIONS
  * "Is this AI?" – The verdict is rules we wrote, so every result can be explained and challenged. The AI only
    explains, and every reply is checked; we show the test where small models gave unsafe advice.
  * "Third-party dependency?" – Every check and verdict is our own code; online lookups and the AI are optional and
    local or cached, and the tool works offline.
  * "False positives?" – Strong signals need backing; one lone signal stays Medium; we show the portfolio-site
    example we fixed. Never "Safe", only "Low risk".
  * "Privacy?" – click-only extension, snip sends only the box, APKs never run, images not stored, synthetic data.
  * "Scale?" – Result cache answers repeats instantly and the heat-list still counts them; SQLite can move to
    PostgreSQL; the API is the same for every channel (WhatsApp next).
