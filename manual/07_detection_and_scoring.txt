07 – DETECTION AND SCORING

SIGNALS
  Each check returns nothing (clear) or the evidence it saw (fired). Every check has:
    tier    H = strong ("hard"), S = weak ("soft"), L = information only (no score)
    weight  how much it adds to the score (strong full weight, weak half)
  Score = sum of fired weights, capped at 100.

RISK LEVELS (core/engine.py verdict)
  Confirmed fraud     an identifier in the input is labelled confirmed fraud by the police. Overrides everything.
  Verified official   all links are on the official registry (and no strong signal fired), or a police label says so.
  Not enough data     fewer than 3 checks could run.
  High                score >= 45 AND one of:
                        - a DECISIVE strong signal (weight >= 45: OTP / PIN request, call-forwarding code,
                          digital arrest, police label, trojan-like APK, QR "receive money" trick…)
                        - two strong signals
                        - one strong signal plus weak signals worth at least 10 points
  Medium              score >= 18, or any strong signal that nothing backs up
  Low                 nothing above. Shown as "Low risk – not a guarantee", never "Safe".
  Why the backing rule: in testing, one wrong strong signal (a portfolio site's "@portfolio" read as a UPI ID) made a
  legitimate site High. Requiring a second signal stops single false alarms; decisive signals still act alone.

IDENTIFIERS AND LINKING
  Found in every input: domains, phone numbers (normalised to +91…), UPI IDs (only with a real UPI handle or UPI
  wording nearby), e-mails, crypto wallets, IPs, Telegram bot tokens, IFSC, GSTIN, APK hash and package.
  Cases sharing an identifier are linked (campaigns, link graph). Two guards against false links:
    - a re-scan of the same site or app is not a "linked case"
    - an earlier High only spreads to other cases if it came from a real signal or a police label
  APK identifiers are cleaned: SDK hosts (Google, Firebase…), Java names, version numbers and placeholder numbers
  are dropped.

DEEP SCAN (optional, needs internet)
  Follows APK links on pages and in messages, and scans servers and e-mails found inside APKs, each as a child case
  (max 3 levels, 12 children). A High child raises the parent ("followed content malicious", decisive).

RESULT CACHE (core/analysis.py)
  Same input (text with spaces normalised, or the same file bytes, link, page HTML, and the same online / deep
  settings) within 12 hours -> the earlier case is returned and counted as a repeat (Times checked, heat-list Seen).
  Not reused when: a police label on one of its identifiers changed, the rules changed (ENGINE_VERSION), the
  officer submitted it, it is a re-scan or a deep-scan child, or "Check again" / fresh=1 is used.

LOOKUP CACHE (core/netcache.py)
  Domain age (RDAP) 7 days ("no date" 6 h), DNS 6 h, fetched pages 6 h. Errors are not cached.
  PS01_NETWORK=cache uses only cached answers (offline venue).

SCREENSHOTS
  QR codes: decoded with OpenCV (always available). UPI QR codes are read (pay / collect, payee, amount).
  Text: Tesseract OCR when installed (English now; Hindi after adding hin.traineddata). Small images are enlarged,
  dark ones inverted. The image itself is never stored; the extracted text is the case input.
